This is the new site. It has been a long time coming.

I have wanted a place that is mine — not a university profile page, not a LinkedIn post, not a PDF buried three clicks deep. Somewhere that holds my research, my CV, and occasionally my thinking, all in one place.

The **Thoughts** section is the part I am most interested in. Academic writing is slow by design: the gap between having an idea and seeing it in print is often two or three years. I want a shorter loop. Not for results — those belong in journals — but for questions, methods, half-formed arguments, and things I find interesting enough to write a paragraph about.

No fixed cadence. No particular audience in mind. Just a place to put things down.

More soon.
